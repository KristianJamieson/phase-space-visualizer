# Explanatory Write-Up: Phase Space Visualizer

This project implements a dynamic visualization tool for autonomous systems of the form 𝑥̇ = f(x), where x ∈ ℝⁿ. The app allows users to input custom nonlinear differential equations, select two-dimensional slices of higher-dimensional systems, and observe the resulting flow field and trajectories. By numerically integrating the system using a fourth-order Runge–Kutta method (RK4), the app provides an intuitive way to study equilibria, limit cycles, and phase-space structure.

This visualization aids in understanding qualitative behavior in nonlinear dynamics—how systems evolve over time, approach stability, or exhibit oscillatory or divergent motion. The examples include canonical models such as the harmonic oscillator, Van der Pol oscillator, and Lotka–Volterra predator–prey system, demonstrating distinct phenomena like circular orbits, limit cycles, and population oscillations. The project highlights how mathematical models and numerical methods combine to reveal the geometry of dynamical systems, directly connecting theory to interactive simulation.
